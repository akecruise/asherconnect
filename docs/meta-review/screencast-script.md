# Screencast Script — Meta App Review

Recommended duration: 1–3 minutes.

Do not show passwords, access tokens, secrets, environment files, developer
console output, or real customer conversations.

## Scene 1 — Messenger

Show the Facebook Messenger test account and the business Page.

Explain:

"This is the test Messenger account that will contact our business Facebook Page."

## Scene 2 — Send test message

Send:

Hello, this is a Meta App Review test message.

Explain:

"The customer sends a Messenger message to our business."

## Scene 3 — APLUS Connect Inbox

Switch to:

https://inbox.apluscondo.com

Log in with the test-only reviewer account.

Open the Inbox.

Explain:

"APLUS Connect receives the Facebook Messenger conversation for our authorized
support and sales staff."

## Scene 4 — Profile information

Open the test conversation and show the sender name.

Explain:

"We use Business Asset User Profile Access to display the Messenger user's
available profile identity next to their customer-service conversation."

## Scene 5 — Reply

Send a short reply from APLUS Connect.

Switch back to Messenger and show the reply.

Explain:

"Our staff can respond to the customer from the same conversation."

## Scene 6 — Reviewer isolation

Return to APLUS Connect.

Explain:

"The App Review account is isolated from production customer data and can access
test conversations only."
