# Use Case — Business Asset User Profile Access

APLUS Connect is an internal customer support and sales inbox used by authorized
staff of our business to manage Facebook Messenger conversations sent to our
business Facebook Page.

## How we use this permission

When a person sends a Messenger message to our Facebook Page, APLUS Connect
receives the conversation through the Meta platform.

We use Business Asset User Profile Access to retrieve the person's display name
and profile information made available by Meta so that the conversation can be
shown to our authorized support or sales staff with a human-identifiable sender.

This information is used only in connection with the person's conversation with
our business.

## Why this is necessary

Without the profile information, staff would see only platform identifiers and
would have difficulty identifying which customer they are assisting.

Displaying the sender's name alongside the Messenger conversation allows staff
to provide customer service, follow up on inquiries, and avoid confusing
different conversations.

## Data usage

APLUS Connect does not use this permission for advertising targeting,
profiling unrelated to customer service, or selling personal information.

Access is restricted to authorized staff.

For App Review, the supplied reviewer account is isolated from production
customer conversations and is limited to test conversations only.
